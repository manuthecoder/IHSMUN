# Welcome letter from the secretary general
We are Jooha Park and Chinmayee Srinivasan, and we are proud to serve as Co-Secretary Generals of the Irvine High School Invitational Model United Nations Conference for the 2026-2027 school year. It is our distinguished honor to welcome you all to our conference this October!
<br />
This year, our conference will be held on a significant date. October 24th is globally recognized as United Nations Day, which commemorates the founding of the United Nations in 1945. This year, the United Nations celebrates 81 years of initiating global reform and diplomatic processes. We also celebrate three years of carrying out the IHSIMUN Conference.
<br />
The IHSIMUN Conference’s theme for this year, The Next Small Step, reflects the historical significance of making fundamental decisions that turn the tides of global affairs, encouraging us to observe how strategic leadership and seemingly small decisions turned into the giant leaps that changed the world as we know it. In this year’s conference, we motivate you to take chances and step out of your comfort zone, so that you can initiate reform and progress.
<br />
We sincerely hope that this conference provides all of you with the opportunity to learn essential skills that will be instrumental in your growth as leaders, diplomats, and citizens, whether that be in your community or on a global scale.
<br />
On behalf of our staff and Secretariat, welcome to the third iteration of Irvine High’s invitational conference; we wish you the very best in growing as future leaders!
<br />
Yours in service,
